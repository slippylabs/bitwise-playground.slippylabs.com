The bits

On a desktop, click any bit of A or B to flip it. Bit 0 is on the right, as it is in every datasheet ever written, and bytes are shaded alternately so you can count them without counting. Thirty-two bits cannot each be a thumb-sized target on a phone, so there the rows are a readout and the boxes above — which take hex, binary, octal or decimal — are how you set a value.

      Shifts and rotates here follow the rules that are actually written down rather than whatever a
